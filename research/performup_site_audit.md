# PerformUp (performup.eu) - Site audit

Audit date: 2026-10-17.

## Method and limits (read first)
- Direct fetching of performup.eu was NOT possible from the sandbox: WebFetch returned `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND performup.eu` (and www.), and curl through the agent proxy returned `403 Forbidden` on CONNECT. No raw HTML was seen, so tracking/tech, forms, footer, legal notices, and exact CTA labels could not be verified.
- Everything below comes from WebSearch result summaries of two indexed pages: `https://performup.eu/` and `https://performup.eu/tests`. Quotes are as relayed by the search tool; treat them as near-verbatim, not guaranteed exact.
- Anything not listed is "not found". All claims are the company's self-reported claims, not independently verified.
- Recommended follow-up: re-run this audit from an unrestricted machine (view-source, Lighthouse, Wayback).

- Navigation (as relayed): **Programs** (Tests, Application, Interviews, Schools) and **Company** (About, Mentors, Results). Content of Application / Interviews / Schools / About / Mentors / Results pages: not found (not indexed/readable).
- Three pillars: tests, application, interviews.
- **Tests** (/tests): GMAT, GRE or Tage Mage prep; diagnostic, personalised prep plan, "30+ hours of 1-on-1 coaching", full exam simulations. Tage Mage described as "the reference admissions exam for French business schools including HEC, ESSEC, and ESCP".
- **Application**: CV, essays, narrative, recommendations; "CV & essays from scratch", positioning and school targeting; documents "written, reviewed and pressure-tested by former jury members".
- **Interviews**: mock interviews run by former jurors or admitted alumni of the student's target school; school-specific prep; live debriefs; bank of "400+ real questions" from actual admission rounds; suggested 1-2 months of interview prep.
- **"Premium Access"** package (named on home): profile audit, school selection, test preparation, application calendar, CV and essay writing (list as relayed; may be incomplete).
- Timeline: "Most students work with us for 4 to 8 months"; suggests starting ~12 months before deadlines.
- Target schools named: HEC, ESSEC, ESCP, INSEAD, LBS, Oxford, Bocconi ("and more"). Full school list: not found.

## 9. Quick weaknesses / gaps observed
- No public pricing; no price anchoring for a likely high-ticket service.
- No independently verifiable social proof: no Trustpilot/Google reviews found; no named testimonials surfaced.
- Team is anonymous in indexed content (no names, LinkedIn links); weak trust signal for a "former jurors" claim.
- "N°1 in Europe", "92%" and "120+ students" lack visible methodology/denominator (92% of what, over which period).
- Refund guarantee conditions not visible in snippets; only a FAQ answer begins "two-way commitment" (need to confirm whether terms are on-page or in a contract).
- Weak brand footprint off-site: brand queries return unrelated companies (PerformanceUp, PerformX, etc.); no social profiles found.
- Competitor article notes no founding date or registered office published (legal transparency gap; mentions légales not verified).
- Thin indexation: only 2 URLs surfaced; no blog found, so little long-tail SEO (e.g. "Tage Mage score HEC", "GMAT prep for ESSEC") while competitors (yourdreamschool.com) publish many such guides.
- English-only (as observed) while Tage Mage and French grandes écoles audience is largely French-speaking; French version not found.
