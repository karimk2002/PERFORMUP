What's going on

The six things that matter most from the research.

Threat

MiM Prep is a near copy of the model

London boutique, master's in management first, GMAT + applications + unlimited mock interviews with alumni, same target schools. Claims 30+ admits to the 2025 LBS MiM class (over 10% of it).

Threat

Hello Prépa owns the social conversation

HEC-alumni founders with influencer reach on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, the same "admis ou remboursé" promise, and packages from €2,290 to €3,890 in eight French cities.

Watch

The refund guarantee is standard in France

Hello Prépa, Admissions Parallèles and Prepfection offer "admis ou remboursé", Prépa Campus "admissible ou remboursé". It only sets PerformUp apart against international firms, where nobody refunds on admission.

Watch

Prices split into three tiers

Paris prépas sell complete programmes at about €2,300 to €4,000. Online challengers undercut at €100 to €1,200. International boutiques charge €6,000 to €10,000 for similar hours.

Opening

Spain and Germany have no local boutique

No Spain-based coaching firm for ESADE or IE masters and no German MiM coaching boutique turned up. Applicants there rely on US or UK firms.

Opening

PerformUp relies on one acquisition channel

The free strategy call is the only way in: no content, free tools, events, ads or third-party reviews.

Tactics worth copying

The moves that bring rivals the most prospects, with who runs them.

A free admission-chances simulator

Students enter their scores and target schools and get a verdict, then a prompt to book a call. No email needed, so it spreads.

Admissions Parallèles (ai-je-mes-chances.fr) · ARINGO · Prepaya

School-by-school SEO pages

One page per school and topic: essays, interview questions, deadlines, cut-off scores. MiM-Essay ranks for HEC, LBS and Bocconi queries with about 1,400 URLs.

MiM-Essay · Prepfection (483 articles) · Admissions Parallèles

A webinar page per origin programme

The same webinar re-titled for each audience: "Intégrer le Top 3 après Dauphine", "après Sciences Po Lille". Each page captures name, email and phone.

Prepfection (about 96 pages) · Training You AST · Prepaya

Founder as creator, plus a community

Build the audience first, then a free Discord or newsletter, then sell. ASTPrep went from TikTok to 4,000+ paying students in two years.

ASTPrep (133K TikTok, 13K Discord) · MiM Prep (55K LinkedIn) · GMAT Ninja

Distribution through partners

Student associations, fair organisers and publishers bring cohorts of prospects at once and lend credibility.

Training You (40+ student associations) · Fortuna × Poets&Quants · Hello Prépa (books, Grand Salon)

Founder video ads with scarcity

MiM Prep runs about 75 Meta ads: founder on camera, "30 places per round", a guarantee and a free training video behind a lead form. No one else targets MiM applicants this way.

MiM Prep · Vincia and Admissions Parallèles on Google search
