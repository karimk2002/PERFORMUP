## 3. Back-of-envelope economics (to check with real numbers)

- **Ticket:** price not public. It sits between Paris prépas (€2.3–4k) and international boutiques (€6–10k), so **assume ~€3.5–5k**.
- **Allowable acquisition cost:** with ~50% gross margin after coach costs, a client can carry **€500–1,000 of acquisition cost**. That is a lot of room for paid ads and partner commissions.
- **Example funnel:** lead → booked call 35% → show 75% → close 25%, so ~6.5% lead→client. At €800 CAC, PerformUp can pay **~€50 per lead**. French student lead forms on Meta usually cost far less (€5–25), so the real issue will be lead **quality**, not cost.
- **Conclusion:** paid acquisition is very likely profitable if the call converts well. **The call is the bottleneck to measure first.**

### Seasonality (key for timing)
- **MiM rounds** (HEC, ESSEC, ESCP, LBS, Bocconi): run from about Oct to Mar. Mid-October is peak decision time for R1/R2 applicants. **Speed matters now.**
- **AST** (admissions parallèles): Tage Mage all year. Applications Jan–Apr, orals Apr–Jun. Students for the 2027 intake start searching around **spring–summer 2026/27**.
- **Plan in two layers:** quick wins for the current cycle, and lasting assets ready for the next one (SEO, simulator, partnerships need 3–6 months).

## 4. Positioning: what can actually sell

- **The guarantee is not a differentiator in France.** Hello Prépa, Admissions Parallèles and Prepfection all offer "admis ou remboursé". It only stands out against international firms.
- **Real differentiators to push:**
  1. **Former jury members.** *Correction, 29 Oct:* this is NOT unique. Hello Prépa, Mission Admission and PGE-PGO claim it in France, and Fortuna, Menlo and Personal MBA Coach claim the international equivalent. It still works as a content angle ("what the jury really thinks of your CV / your answer"), but it only stands out with **proof**: named coaches, faces, results by school.
  2. **France + international in one place.** AST/MiM and LBS/Bocconi/GMAT together. Only MyCursus does this.
  3. **Fully 1:1 and unlimited mock interviews.** Paris prépas sell groups.
- **Main threats:** MiM Prep (UK, same model, ~75 founder video ads on Meta, "30 places per round") and Hello Prépa (influencer reach).
- **Hole in the offer:** no price shown, no third-party reviews. Both cost conversions on every channel we might switch on.

## 6. Shortlist for the proposal (90-day pilot)

The logic is to **fix the bucket, open one fast tap, and build one asset**, all **around Simo's outbound, not instead of it**. The story for the call: "Your outbound brings attention. Today, part of it is lost on the way to a signed client, and there is no second source of leads when outbound slows down. I build that second source and the trust layer, and we measure everything together."

1. **Weeks 1–2: Diagnostic + plumbing (A1, A3, tracking).** Get Simo's numbers. Put a CRM, speed-to-lead WhatsApp and follow-ups in place. Install pixel and GA4. Launch the reviews drive (A2). → Measurable baseline. This alone usually lifts conversion.
2. **Weeks 2–10: Paid test on the current cycle (B1 + B2 + B4).** Founder/jury videos with an instant lead form, high-intent Google keywords, and a "refusé au R1" angle. Budget €1.5–3k/month. Kill or scale based on cost per signed client.
3. **Weeks 3–8: Build the simulator (C1).** One lasting asset that feeds ads, SEO and partnerships, and sets PerformUp apart on the "jury" angle.
4. **Run alongside, low effort: referral + ambassadors (D2), 5–10 student associations (D1) and signal-based lists for Simo's outbound (D5).** These are close to what Simo does already, so he runs them with lists and templates Karim prepares.
5. **Months 3–6 (if the pilot works):** SEO school pages (C2), webinars per feeder programme (C5), Maghreb partnerships (D3).

**Pilot KPIs:** leads/week, booked-call rate, show rate, close rate, cost per signed client (CAC), revenue attributed, review count.

## 7. How Karim could be paid (options to choose from)

| Model | What | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Paid diagnostic** | 2–3 weeks: funnel audit + market data + 90-day plan. Fixed fee (e.g. €1–2.5k), deducted if they continue. | Low risk for them, values the research already done | Revenue stops there if they stop |
| **2. Build + launch** | Fixed project fee for the simulator + CRM/automations + ad setup | Clear scope, fits Karim's limited hours | No upside if results are big |
| **3. Retainer + success fee** *(preferred)* | Small monthly base (e.g. €800–1.5k) + fee per signed client from tracked channels (e.g. €200–400, or 8–10% of the ticket) | Aligned incentives, sustainable, shows confidence | Needs clean attribution (CRM + UTMs from day 1) |
| **4. Pure performance / rev share** | Only paid per client | Easy yes for them | High risk for Karim, depends on their close rate, which he does not control |

**Recommendation:** propose a **90-day pilot in model 3**, with the diagnostic included in month 1. Exit clause after 90 days for both sides. Keep the option to talk about equity/advisor status later if it becomes a real growth role.

**Framing with Simo:** Simo owns GTM and is strong on outbound and automation, so **don't step on that**. Split the work clearly:
- **Simo:** outbound, partnerships, relationships (associations, ambassadors).
- **Karim:** inbound (paid + organic), trust and conversion assets (FR site, reviews, simulator, content system), tracking and attribution.
- **Together:** a weekly 30-minute review of the numbers.

The success fee only counts clients from **Karim's channels** (inbound, tracked by UTM/CRM), so nobody argues over outbound credit.
