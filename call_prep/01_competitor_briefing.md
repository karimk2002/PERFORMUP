## 1. The market in one minute

**53 firms go after the same applicants** (HEC, ESSEC, ESCP, LBS, LSE, Bocconi…). They fall into 4 families:

| Family | Who | Price of a full programme | How they sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **French AST / Tage Mage prépas** (14) | Hello Prépa, Ipesup, Admissions Parallèles, Prepfection, Prepaya, Prépa Laurea, Prépa Campus… | **€1,500–4,000** (groups + some 1:1) | SEO, free mocks/simulators, webinars, Google reviews, Google ads |
| **International MiM/MBA consultancies** (11) | MiM Prep, Fortuna, Menlo, ARINGO, Personal MBA Coach, MiM-Essay… | **€2,000–10,000+** | GMAT Club / Poets&Quants rankings, LinkedIn, founder content, Meta ads |
| **Other European boutiques** (12) | Leadearly (UK), Crimson, UNI Student Advisors and Omni (Italy), The Profs… | Mostly not public | Fairs, partnerships, PR, SEO |
| **GMAT/GRE test prep** (12) | Manhattan Prep, Target Test Prep, e-GMAT, Vincia, GMAT Ninja… | €100–6,000 | Free trials, GMAT Club, Google ads |

**Three price tiers:** online challengers at €100–1,200, Paris prépas at about €2,300–4,000, international boutiques at €6,000–10,000. **PerformUp doesn't show a price.** With 30h of 1:1 + application + interviews, it logically sits between the prépas and the international boutiques.

## 2. Five things to know (and say)

1. **"Admis ou remboursé" is standard in France.** Hello Prépa, Admissions Parallèles and Prepfection offer it; Prépa Campus offers "admissible ou remboursé". It only stands out against the international firms, where nobody refunds.
2. **"Former jury members" is not unique either.** Hello Prépa ("official interview jurors"), Mission Admission (founder is an HEC jury member) and PGE-PGO ("active jury members") say it in France. Fortuna, Menlo and Personal MBA Coach (former admissions directors) say it internationally.
   → **No single claim of PerformUp is unique. What can make the difference is *proof* (named results, reviews, faces) and *visibility*.** This is the core argument for Karim's offer.
3. **The closest copy is MiM Prep (London).** Same model: test + application + unlimited mock interviews, same schools, also about 120 students a year. It runs **~75 Meta ads** (founder on camera, "30 places per round", a free training video behind a form) plus 55K LinkedIn followers. **Nobody does this in French yet.**
4. **The leaders get clients through free tools and content, not cold outreach.** Rivals use **6.8 of 11 channels on average**. The ones that work best:
   - **Admission-chances simulator:** Admissions Parallèles (ai-je-mes-chances.fr), Prepaya, ARINGO.
   - **One SEO page per school:** Prepfection (483 articles), MiM-Essay (~1,400 URLs).
   - **One webinar page per origin programme:** Prepfection (~96 pages, e.g. "Top 3 après Dauphine").
   - **Founder as creator, plus a community:** ASTPrep went from TikTok to 4,000+ paying students in two years (133K TikTok, 13K Discord).
   - **Student associations as distribution:** Training You (40+ associations).
   - **Reviews as proof:** Prépa Campus (184 at 5.0), Your Dream School (~162), Prépa Laurea (100% 5-star).
5. **Who pays for ads (and what that tells us):**
   - **Google search:** Ipesup ~400 ads, Prépa Laurea ~200, Admissions Parallèles ~40, Vincia ~36. People who search "prépa AST" or "préparation oral" are the warmest leads.
   - **Meta:** MiM Prep ~75, Your Dream School ~36 (**French copy aimed at parents**), Ipesup ~22.
   - **PerformUp:** 0 on Meta, Google and LinkedIn.

## 3. What we can't see: outbound

None of this research can see LinkedIn outbound, and **no competitor visibly leans on it**. So Simo's channel may be **a real edge that nobody copies**. It also has limits:
- **A ceiling:** LinkedIn caps invitations at roughly 100–200 a week per account, and the work depends on one person.
- **The site doesn't back it up:** a prospect who clicks lands on a site that seems English-only, with an anonymous team, no reviews and no price.
- **No second engine:** when outbound slows down, or in peak season, there is nothing else to fall back on.

## 5. Gaps in the market PerformUp can take

- **French-language founder/jury video ads aimed at MiM applicants:** nobody does this in French.
- **Parents as buyers:** only Your Dream School targets them, and they are the ones who pay €3–5k.
- **International MiM applicants who want a refund guarantee:** no international firm offers one.
- **Spain and Germany:** no local boutique for ESADE or IE masters, and no German MiM coaching boutique. Applicants there use US or UK firms.
- **Francophone students abroad** (Morocco, Tunisia, Lebanon, West Africa) applying to French schools: barely served.
- **An "AST + international" 1:1 offer at a mid price:** only MyCursus is near it.
