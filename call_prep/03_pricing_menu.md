# Full pricing menu: inbound beyond ads

Companion to `00_offer_reasoning.md`. That file priced the paid-ads pilot; this one prices **everything else in inbound**: foundations, SEO content, organic social content, lead magnets, nurturing, webinars.

All prices excl. VAT. Market rates are rough French freelance ranges, used as anchors, not quotes.

---

## 1. Principles behind the prices

1. **PerformUp is small** (~€150–300k revenue/year, an estimate). Total spend with Karim should stay around **€1–2k/month**, which is less than one signed client a month. The pitch: "it pays for itself with 1 extra client a month".
2. **Organic is slow and hard to attribute.** SEO takes 3–6 months; content builds trust more than direct sign-ups. So organic is **mostly fixed-fee**, with a **6-month minimum for SEO**. A success fee alone would make Karim wait half a year to get paid.
3. **Karim has 4–6 h/week.** Each line below shows the hours it takes. **He can't sell everything at once:** at most foundations + **2 recurring engines**, or he subcontracts (writer, video editor) and keeps a margin.
4. **AI makes Karim faster, and the price reflects the output, not the hours.** Karim drafts with AI; coaches check the substance, which is their expertise and part of the deal; Karim edits and publishes.
5. **PerformUp provides** the expertise (coach review, 30 min per batch), the faces (Sara/coaches on video), and site access.

### C. Variable part (on top of any recurring engine)

- **€200–300 per signed client** whose first touch is one of Karim's channels (SEO page, simulator, lead magnet, webinar, organic social link, ads), tracked by UTMs + CRM. Outbound clients stay Simo's.
- This is lower than the €300–400 in the ads-only pilot, because the fixed fees are higher here.
- **Alternative if they prefer low fixed fees:** cut every monthly price by ~30% and raise the bonus to €400–500 per client.

## 4. What to recommend tomorrow

**Lead with "Fondations" as the first step, then let them choose 1–2 engines.** Reasons:
- It **fixes what hurts Simo's outbound today**: prospects who click land on an English-only, anonymous site with no reviews. He benefits from day one, so he won't see Karim as a rival.
- It is concrete, fast (5 weeks) and low-risk, and **one client pays for it**.
- Every later engine (SEO, social, ads) needs it anyway: tracking, a French landing page, proof.
- It lets Karim prove himself before a monthly commitment.

**Then for the engine:** SEO (B1) if they think long term and have no ad budget. Ads (B3) if they want clients this season (MiM rounds until March). Social (B2) only if Sara is truly willing to film every month. Otherwise it fails, so ask her directly.

**On the call, say it like this:**
« Je te propose de commencer par les fondations : page en français, avis, suivi des leads, un aimant à leads avec vos 400 questions d'entretien. C'est 3 000 €, payable en trois fois, et un seul client signé le rembourse. Ensuite, on choisit ensemble un ou deux moteurs : le SEO école par école, le contenu avec Sara, ou la pub, selon votre budget et votre horizon. »

## 5. Watch-outs

- **Time:** bundle 3 is at the edge of 6–7 h/week. If they want everything, subcontract the writing or editing and keep a margin of about 30%, or phase it (SEO first, ads in month 3).
- **Scope creep:** write down the deliverables per month (number of pages, scripts, revisions: 1 round). Extra work at a daily rate of about €400.
- **Payment terms:** foundations 50% upfront; monthly fees paid at the start of each month; bonus invoiced monthly from the CRM.
- **Ownership:** site, content, accounts and data belong to PerformUp. Karim keeps the right to cite the case (anonymised if they prefer).
- **Revo:** check the side-work clause before signing anything.
