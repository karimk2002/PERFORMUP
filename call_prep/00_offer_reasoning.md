# What to offer Simo: reasoning + call plan

Call: 30 Oct 2026, morning. Competitor context: `01_competitor_briefing.md`.

---

## 1. The situation in five facts

1. **Simo owns GTM and is an outbound + automation person.** LinkedIn prospecting is how PerformUp gets clients today. He asked for **a plan they can put in place, presented on a call and then sent in writing.**
2. **PerformUp is small.** It claims 120+ students coached in total. At an assumed €3.5–5k ticket, that is probably **€150–300k a year in revenue**. A big fixed retainer would be a real cost for them.
3. **Karim works full time at Revo.** Realistically 4–6 h/week after the setup. His track record is in food and hospitality, not education, so **his best proof here is the research**: he already knows their market better than they do.
4. **Their weak spots are trust and inbound,** not outbound: a site that seems English-only, an anonymous team, no reviews, no price, no ads, no content, no free tool.
5. **The timing is good:** MiM rounds run October to March, and AST students for the 2027 intake start looking in spring. Anything built now pays off this season *and* the next.

## 2. What to offer, and what not to

**Offer:** the **inbound + trust layer around Simo's outbound**, and the **tracking** that shows what works.
- Trust and conversion: a French landing page/site, named coaches with photos, results by school, a reviews drive (Google + Trustpilot + video testimonials), WhatsApp follow-up within 5 minutes.
- Paid inbound: Meta founder/jury video ads with an instant lead form (students **and parents**), plus Google search on high-intent keywords.
- One asset that lasts: the "Quelles sont mes chances ?" simulator or the 400-question interview bank as a lead magnet. It feeds ads and SEO, and gives Simo warm signals for his outbound.
- Tracking: CRM pipeline, UTMs, a weekly KPI review (leads → calls → shows → signed → cost per client).

**Don't offer:**
- Running his outbound. That's his turf; at most, hand him signal-based lists.
- Being on sales calls.
- Daily community management or open-ended content production.
- A results guarantee without an ad budget and without control over how the call converts.

## 3. Three options to put on the table

> **Update 29 Oct:** the options below cover the **paid-ads** offer only. The full menu, with organic content, SEO, simulator, lead magnet, nurturing, webinars and bundles, is in **`03_pricing_menu.md`**. **New recommendation:** lead with the **"Fondations" one-off (€3,000, 5 weeks)**, then let them pick 1–2 recurring engines (SEO, social content or ads). The ads pilot below becomes one of those engines.

Present them as a menu, with the recommended one first. Give the structure and ranges on the call; confirm exact numbers in the written proposal after seeing their figures.

| | **A. 90-day growth pilot** *(recommended)* | **B. Pay on results** | **C. Setup sprint only** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | Setup (tracking, FR landing page, reviews, ad accounts, lead form → WhatsApp) + run the Meta/Google tests + build the simulator or lead magnet. Weekly 30-min review | Same scope as A | 3–4 weeks: tracking/CRM, FR landing page, reviews drive, ads and automations set up. Then Simo runs it |
| Fixed | **€500–800 / month** | €0 | **€1.5–2.5k one-off** |
| Variable | **€300–400 per signed client** from Karim's channels (≈ 8–10% of the ticket) | **12–15% of revenue** from Karim's channels, for 12 months | — |
| Ad budget (paid by them, direct) | min. **€1k/month** for 3 months | min. €1k/month, committed | Their choice |
| Good for | Both sides: fair, aligned, low risk | Them if cash is tight; Karim if their close rate is good | Them if Simo wants to execute himself |
| Risk for Karim | Low | High: depends on their call and on attribution | No upside |

**Why these numbers:**
- A French freelance growth marketer charges about €400–600 a day. 4–6 h/week is roughly 3–4 days a month, or €1.2–2.4k. **A €500–800 base is a "pilot discount"** that is easy to say yes to.
- The success fee is what makes it worthwhile. Example: 10 signed clients in 3 months gives €3–4k variable plus €1.5–2.4k fixed, so **€5–6k for about 65 hours**.
- **Attribution rule:** only clients whose first touch is one of Karim's channels count (ad lead form, simulator, Google, inbound site form, tracked with UTMs + CRM). Outbound clients stay Simo's. This avoids any conflict over who brought whom.

**Conditions to ask for, whatever the option:**
1. Ad budget committed for 3 months. Below €1k/month, the test can't tell anything.
2. Access to the CRM / sales numbers. Without the close rate, no one can judge the result.
3. Leads answered within 1 hour on working days, by them.
4. 2–3 hours of Sara's or the coaches' time for video filming, plus their help collecting reviews from past students.
5. Checkpoints at day 45 and day 90. Either side can stop at day 90.

**How to decide during the call:**
- Ticket ≥ €3.5k **and** an ad budget is available → **A**.
- No ad budget → offer **A's organic version** (reviews + FR page + simulator + lead magnet, no paid ads) at the same base, or **B**. But B only makes sense with some budget behind it, otherwise the funnel stays empty.
- "Thanks, we'll do it ourselves" → **C**. It still pays for the setup, and keeps the door open.
- Later, if it works: talk about an ongoing role (advisor, % of inbound revenue, or equity). **Don't raise equity on this first call.**

**Before signing:** check the Revo contract for side-work, exclusivity or non-compete clauses.

## 4. What to send after the call (what Simo asked for)

A short French document (3–4 pages), within 48 h:
1. What I understood (their numbers and goals).
2. The market in 5 points (from the briefing).
3. The 90-day plan: 3 workstreams, with a week-by-week timeline.
4. KPIs and the attribution rule.
5. The chosen option, its price and conditions.

**Keep the "how" for after signing.** Exact ad set-ups, creatives and the simulator build are the paid part. The plan shows *what* and *why* in enough detail to be credible, without being a free playbook.
